# JNTU CSE 2nd Year Question Paper Portal

## 📚 Project Overview

The **JNTU CSE 2nd Year Question Paper Portal** is a web-based educational platform designed to help Computer Science and Engineering students prepare for semester examinations, laboratory programs, and placement interviews.

The portal organizes study material in a structured manner such as:

**Subject → Unit → Topic → Questions**

It provides students with easy access to previous question papers, short-answer questions, long-answer questions, laboratory programs, and placement preparation materials.

---

## 🎯 Objectives

- Provide a single platform for JNTU CSE 2nd year preparation.
- Organize questions subject-wise, unit-wise, and topic-wise.
- Provide Part-A short questions and Part-B long questions.
- Provide laboratory programs separately.
- Provide placement preparation materials.
- Reduce the time required to search for study material.
- Provide a simple and user-friendly interface.
- Store and manage questions using a centralized database.
